01 — Research

Connecting protein behaviour to measurable biophysical properties.

For six months, I independently investigated the induction and properties of liquid-liquid phase separation of α-synuclein. The project combined wet-lab experimentation with quantitative imaging and biophysical analysis.

I planned experiments, optimised protocols, collected and analysed data, troubleshot experimental issues and interpreted results across several complementary techniques.
