01 — Overview

Quantitative modelling for translational oncology.

This master research project focuses on pharmacometric population modelling and simulation of temozolomide exposure in glioblastoma. The work combines clinical concentration data with mechanistic and statistical modelling approaches.

I work with R, nlmixr2, rxode2 and NONMEM, including model development, simulation, trial design and power calculations.
